# Citations and research resources

## Required

Attach the citation to the claim it supports. Identify title, authors/year where useful, and a canonical DOI or publisher/author-hosted source. Distinguish paper, supplementary material, preprint, wiki explanation, and code recreation. Do not call a preprint peer reviewed without evidence.

Do not fabricate page numbers, theorem statements, quotes, bibliographic fields, or claims of having read a paper. A locally recorded citation establishes provenance of the reference, not that its contents were freshly inspected. Respect a no-browsing instruction; use available material and identify gaps rather than fetching more sources.

## Previously recorded research starting points

These were linked in the project's [technical report](../../../../docs/pluribus_solver_technical_report.md); choose only sources relevant to the changed content. They have not been newly live-checked by this skill.

- [Brown and Sandholm, Superhuman AI for multiplayer poker (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aay2400): original Pluribus research and reported results, not local TexasSolver validation.
- [Pluribus supplementary material](https://noambrown.com/papers/19-Science-Superhuman_Supp.pdf): algorithmic details accompanying the original paper.
- [Solving Imperfect-Information Games via Discounted Regret Minimization](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v33i01.33011829): discounted regret-minimization background.
- [Depth-Limited Solving for Imperfect-Information Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08195): depth-limited search background; identify the linked version accurately.
- [Safe and Nested Subgame Solving for Imperfect-Information Games](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.02955): subgame-solving concepts and their assumptions.
- [Potential-Aware Imperfect-Recall Abstraction](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v28i1.8816): abstraction background.
- [Action Translation and the Pseudo-Harmonic Mapping](https://www.ijcai.org/Abstract/13/028): action-translation background.
- [Lossless Abstraction of Imperfect Information Games](https://doi.org/10.1145/1284320.1284324): abstraction theory.

## Tips

A short root bibliography can link a deeper maintained reference list. Wiki pages and accessible explainers can help orientation, but should not replace primary research when asserting algorithmic guarantees. Cite external recreation results as that recreation's claims.
